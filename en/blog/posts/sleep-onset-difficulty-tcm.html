Difficulty falling asleep: the more you think about it, the more you wake up? Traditional Chinese Medicine starts from mind and rhythm

The body is very tired but the mind can't stop after lying in bed, and the more late at night, the more energetic it becomes. Difficulty falling asleep does not necessarily mean "severe insomnia", but if it exists for a long time, it will affect the mood and overall state. Traditional Chinese medicine believes that this is often the regulating signal sent by the mind, qi and blood, and the rhythm of yin and yang.

January 14, 2026 · guoyitang11366@gmail.com · Health column

Many people have this problem: they are obviously very tired, but they feel more and more sleepy when they lie in bed; their minds cannot stop and turn over and over, and they even become more energetic as the night goes on.Difficulty falling asleepIt does not mean "severe insomnia", but if it persists for a long time, it will gradually affect mood, immunity and overall health. Traditional Chinese medicine believes that being unable to sleep is never a simple "brain problem";The body is sending out regulatory signals。

1. How does Chinese medicine view "difficulty falling asleep"? ——Uneasiness is the key

Traditional Chinese medicine attributes sleep tomind. "Nei Jing" says:"When yang enters yin, it is sleepy; when yang comes out of yin, it is sleepy."It means that only when the Yang Qi is hidden smoothly and the mind is at peace, can people fall asleep; if the mind is restless and the Qi and blood are imbalanced, it will be difficult for sleepiness to come naturally. Difficulty falling asleep is often related to factors such as lack of nourishment of the mind and spirit, discomfort in the liver qi, insufficient qi and blood, imbalance of yin and yang, etc. It is necessary to combine the four diagnostic methods. It is not advisable to abuse medicated foods or patent medicines after sitting down on your own.

2. Several common types of difficulty falling asleep (popular science comparison, non-diagnosis)

The following is a rough summary for easy understanding,It cannot replace the diagnosis of a doctor：

1. Too many thoughts and can’t stop

Features:Thinking about things after lying down, the more you want to relax, the more awake you will be, which may lead to anxiety and stress.Common syndrome differentiation ideas:Liver stagnation, restlessness, etc.

2. You are obviously very tired, but you can’t sleep.

Features:I feel tired during the day, wake up at night, and feel restless after sleeping.Common syndrome differentiation ideas:Insufficient Qi and blood, loss of spiritual support, etc.

3. Easy to wake up and light sleeper

Features:Waking up just after falling asleep, being sensitive to sounds, and having many dreams.Common syndrome differentiation ideas:Heart and gallbladder qi deficiency, heart and kidney disharmony, etc.

4. Difficulty falling asleep with dry mouth and irritability

Features:Feeling upset when lying down, dry mouth, easy to wake up at night.Common syndrome differentiation ideas:Yin deficiency, internal heat, etc.

3. The core ideas of traditional Chinese medicine in treating difficulty falling asleep

Traditional Chinese medicine conditioning is not simply "forcing sleep aid", but helps the body restore a more natural sleep rhythm. Common directions include:

  • Calming the mind and calming the mind:Make the mind attached.
  • Reconciling Qi and Blood:As qi and blood gradually become fuller, the mind becomes more peaceful and sleep becomes easier.
  • Soothe the liver and relieve depression:When your Qi is smooth, your thoughts will be relatively easy to calm down.
  • Regulate the rhythm of yin and yang:Help Yang Qi enter Yin smoothly.

The specific governance law must be determined byLicensed Chinese PhysicianConfirmed after face-to-face consultation.

4. TCM methods for regulating sleep that can be done daily

1. “Slow down” before going to bed

People who have difficulty falling asleep are often not sleepy, but unable to stop. It is recommended that you stop processing work information about 1 hour before going to bed, browse less stimulating content, and gradually dim the lights to allow your body to gradually enter a resting rhythm.

2. Soak your feet in warm water

Soaking your feet in warm water for about 15-20 minutes can help relax and improve the sleep experience for some people with cold hands and feet. You can add a small amount of mugwort leaves or ginger slices (Use with caution if you have a hot constitution, damaged skin, diabetic foot, etc., it is advisable to consult a physician first).

3. Simple acupoint massage

Before going to bed, you can gently massage Shenmen, Neiguan, Baihui, etc. for about 1-2 minutes on each acupoint, and the intensity should be as comfortable as possible.

4. Eat light before going to bed

Dinner should not be too full; avoid spicy food, excessive alcohol, strong tea and coffee; try to avoid eating large amounts about 2 hours before going to bed to avoid stomach disharmony and restless sleep.

5. TCM conditioning methods (professional syndrome differentiation required)

If difficulty falling asleep persists, it may be considered under the evaluation of a physicianAcupuncture(Reconciling the heart, liver and kidneys, adjusting sleep rhythm),traditional Chinese medicine(Supplementing qi and blood, nourishing heart yin, soothing the liver, etc. can be added or subtracted according to the syndrome),Massage or moxibustion(Relax tension) etc. The goal of conditioning is usually not to "fall asleep immediately", but rather to make sleepiness appear more naturally and the process of falling asleep gradually become smoother.

6. When should further evaluation be conducted?

If the following situations occur, it is recommended to consult as soon as possibleMedical practitioner or psychiatrist, and can simultaneously seekLicensed Chinese PhysicianSyndrome differentiation and conditioning:

  • Difficulty falling asleep persists≥ about 3 weeks
  • accompanied by significant anxiety or depression
  • Sleep problems have significantly affected work and life during the day
  • Patients with heart palpitations, chest tightness, frequent awakenings at night, etc. who need to rule out organic diseases

Long-term neglect may sometimes progress from "difficulty falling asleep" to an overall sleep disorder, so early intervention is recommended.

Conclusion

Difficulty falling asleep is often not due to lack of willpower, but more likeRhythm and mind need to be readjustedsignal. When the mind gradually calms down, the qi and blood are harmonious, and the yang energy can be smoothly hidden, sleep can gradually return to a more stable state.
