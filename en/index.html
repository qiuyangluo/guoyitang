Moxibustion

Moxibustion is one of the traditional therapies of traditional Chinese medicine. The warmth generated by burning mugwort stimulates specific acupuncture points, dredges meridians, warms yang and replenishes qi, removes cold and dampness, and stimulates the body's self-healing ability.

Acupuncture

Acupuncture is one of the classic therapies of traditional Chinese medicine. It can not only relieve pain, but also regulate the immune system and chronic diseases.
