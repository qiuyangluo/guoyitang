Calendar draft (ICS + email)

Visitors do not log in with Google here. Use this helper to summarize a tentative time: the page downloads an .ics file (import into Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple Calendar) and opens a draft email to the clinic. Staff confirms and adds the booking to your team calendar.

Why not auto-sync? Writing events directly into Google Calendar requires Calendar API credentials on a server (for example a Vercel function with a service account authorized on that calendar). A static website alone cannot impersonate your calendar.

Embedded view (requires the calendar to allow public embed or viewer permissions): Open embed · Google Calendar UI (administration usually requires sign-in).
