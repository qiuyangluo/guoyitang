Guoyitang’s characteristic traditional Chinese medicine method—moxibustion

Warm up healthy energy and stimulate self-healing power

Moxibustion is one of the traditional therapies of traditional Chinese medicine. The warmth generated by burning mugwort stimulates specific acupuncture points, dredges meridians, warms yang and replenishes qi, removes cold and dampness, and stimulates the body's self-healing ability. Guoyitang combines modern medical concepts to optimize traditional moxibustion therapy to make it safer and more efficient, making it an important choice for treating chronic diseases and improving physical fitness.
1. The unique advantages of moxibustion
  • Warming the meridians, dispersing cold, and reconciling yin and yang: The warming effect of moxibustion can penetrate deep into the internal organs, and is especially suitable for various discomforts caused by cold constitution, insufficient qi and blood, and meridian blockage, such as uterine cold, joint pain, indigestion, etc. in women.
  • Stimulate healthy qi and improve immunity: Moxibustion not only warms and replenishes yang qi, but also improves immune function and enhances the body's disease resistance. It is suitable for people who are weak and prone to colds, sub-healthy people and patients with chronic fatigue syndrome.
  • Non-invasive, natural therapy: Compared with drug treatment, moxibustion is an external treatment method that improves physical fitness through thermal stimulation of acupoints. It does not require medication and is suitable for long-term conditioning.
  • Modern improvement, safer and more convenient: Guoyitang uses smokeless moxibustion to avoid the risk of traditional moxibustion with high smoke, making it more acceptable to modern people.
2. Complementarity between moxibustion and modern medicine

Moxibustion is more suitable for chronic diseases, immune function regulation and rehabilitation management. •Moxibustion focuses on overall conditioning: it can promote qi and blood circulation, improve symptoms of chronic diseases, and enhance the body's recovery ability, such as postoperative rehabilitation, chronic disease management, dysmenorrhea, etc.

3. Applicable people
  • Moxibustion is suitable for a variety of chronic diseases and sub-health states. Guoyitang is especially recommended to the following groups:
  • Gynecological diseases: various gynecological pains, irregular menstruation, cold hands and feet, fear of cold, and easy cold;
  • Patients with chronic diseases: neck, shoulder, waist and leg pain, indigestion, gastrointestinal disorders, insomnia and anxiety;
  • Low immunity: COVID-19 sequelae, fatigue syndrome, and repeated episodes of chronic inflammation;
  • Sub-healthy people: chronic fatigue, lack of energy, anxiety, insomnia, and excessive stress.
4. Scientific moxibustion, safe and effective

•Choose appropriate moxibustion methods according to your physical constitution, such as mild moxibustion, ginger-partition moxibustion, Yang-supporting moxibustion, keel-bone moxibustion, etc., to avoid burns and excessive moxibustion. •Moxibustion can provide long-term conditioning, but a personalized plan needs to be carried out under the guidance of a professional doctor.

5. Modern moxibustion makes traditional Chinese medicine more relevant to life

Guoyitang's moxibustion therapy combines traditional moxibustion techniques with modern equipment to make moxibustion more efficient, safer and more convenient, allowing more people to benefit from this ancient and scientific traditional Chinese medicine method, helping to maintain health, warm and nourish righteousness, and improve the quality of life.

  • Chronic pelvic inflammatory disease: By clearing away heat and dampness, removing blood stasis and dispersing stagnation, cupping can effectively relieve abdominal pain and abnormal leucorrhea caused by chronic inflammation.
  • Postpartum conditioning: Cupping can help dispel wind and cold, restore Qi and blood, promote postpartum uterine recovery, and relieve back pain.
2. Application of cupping in common diseases
  • Cupping can promote immunity and regulate organ function, and has unique advantages in treating common seasonal diseases:
  • COVID-19 sequelae: For long-term cough, shortness of breath, fatigue, and chest tightness, cupping can clear the lungs, resolve phlegm, and enhance immunity.
  • Chronic bronchitis and allergic rhinitis: Cupping can dispel wind and relieve symptoms, warm yang and strengthen the surface. It is suitable for people with repeated colds, nasal congestion and cough.
  • Indigestion, stomachache, constipation: By stimulating acupoints related to the digestive system, cupping can regulate gastrointestinal motility and relieve indigestion and bloating.
  • Neck, shoulder, waist and leg pain: Cupping can activate blood circulation and remove blood stasis, relax tendons and unblock meridians, and relieve shoulder and neck stiffness, lumbar disc herniation, and arthritis.
  • Insomnia, anxiety, neurasthenia: For long-term mental stress and sleep disorders, cupping can calm and soothe the mind.
3. Canning methods and precautions
  • Guoyitang provides a variety of cupping techniques to choose appropriate treatments for different conditions:
  • Keep the pot: It is suitable for cold-damp constitution, uterine cold, and arthritis. It can quickly remove cold and blood stasis.
  • Walking cup: used for back muscle soreness, stiff shoulders and necks, and chronic bronchitis. It can promote Qi and blood circulation.
  • Flash tank: suitable for people with weak constitutions, can precisely control the adsorption force, is safe and painless. Guoyitang’s characteristic traditional Chinese medicine method—Gua Sha

Stimulates menstruation, activates blood circulation, removes dampness and detoxifies

Gua Sha is one of the traditional external treatments of traditional Chinese medicine. By scraping the skin of specific parts, it stimulates the meridians and acupuncture points to promote the circulation of qi and blood, detoxify and remove dampness, and relieve fatigue. Guoyitang combines modern rehabilitation concepts to make scraping therapy safer and more scientific. It is especially suitable for traditional Chinese medicine gynecological conditioning and the prevention and treatment of common seasonal diseases, helping women to take care of their bodies and improve their immunity.
1. Application of Gua Sha in TCM Gynecology
  • Traditional Chinese medicine believes that women's health is closely related to the circulation of qi and blood, the function of the internal organs, and the constitution of cold and dampness. Gua Sha can reconcile qi and blood, warm the menstrual flow and dispel cold, activate blood circulation and remove blood stasis, and has significant effects on the following gynecological diseases:
  • Irregular menstruation (dysmenorrhea, amenorrhea, scanty menstruation) - regulates Qi and blood, warms menstruation and relieves pain.
  • Infertility caused by uterine cold (cold uterus, ovulation disorder) - Warm and nourish kidney yang to increase the pregnancy rate.
  • Menopausal syndrome (hot flashes, night sweats, heart palpitations, insomnia, emotional instability) - regulates endocrine, soothes the liver and regulates qi.
  • Postpartum recovery (fatigue, lochia) - Promote metabolism and help the body recover.
  • Chronic pelvic inflammatory disease (abnormal leucorrhea, lower abdominal pain) - clear away heat and dampness, reduce inflammation and remove blood stasis.
2. Application of Gua Sha in common diseases
  • Neck, shoulder, lower back pain (cervical spondylosis, frozen shoulder, lumbar disc herniation) - relax muscles and dredge meridians, activate blood circulation and relieve pain.
  • Gua Sha can stimulate acupuncture points, adjust organ functions, enhance immunity, and has a good regulating effect on common diseases of the season:
  • Sequelae of COVID-19 (persistent cough, chest tightness and fatigue) - Clear the lungs and resolve phlegm to relieve shortness of breath.
  • Chronic bronchitis, allergic rhinitis (easy to catch colds, coughs and asthma) - expels cold and relieves the lungs, improves immunity.
  • Indigestion, stomach pain, constipation (poor appetite, bloating) - strengthens the spleen and stomach and promotes intestinal peristalsis.
  • Insomnia, anxiety, neurasthenia (light sleep, easy awakening, dreaminess) - calms the nerves and relieves tension.
3. Gua Sha method and safety

Guoyitang provides different scraping methods and develops personalized plans for different conditions. The skin may develop bruises (ecchymoses) after scraping, which will usually subside naturally in 3-5 days. It is recommended to avoid direct wind and cold, keep warm and hydrate appropriately. Pregnant women, extremely weak people, and people with high levels of anemia are not suitable for Gua Sha.
