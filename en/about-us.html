about Us

Guoyitang, New York | GUOYITANG – Chinese Medicine Clinic in Flushing, New York

New York Guoyitang was co-founded by a number of New York TCM experts with more than 30 years of clinical experience, focusing on acupuncture, traditional Chinese medicine conditioning, andrology and gynecology systematic diagnosis and treatment.

As a professional Chinese medicine clinic in Flushing, New York, Guoyitang combines traditional Chinese medicine syndrome differentiation and treatment with modern medical concepts to provide systematic and personalized Chinese medicine health management services to patients in New York and surrounding areas.

New York Guoyitang (GUOYITANG) was established in 2019 and is located on the first floor of the Huamei Building in Flushing, Queens, New York City. It has convenient transportation, a quiet and comfortable environment, and is one of the most trusted traditional Chinese medicine clinics in New York.

The clinic is committed to promoting the culture of traditional Chinese medicine and the concept of overall health in New York, focusing on "regulating the body and balancing yin and yang" to help patients improve chronic diseases and sub-health conditions.
